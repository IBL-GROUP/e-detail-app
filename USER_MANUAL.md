# Searle E-Detailing — Mobile App User Manual

**For Medical Representatives (MIEs)**

This guide explains how to use the Searle E-Detailing app on your phone, step by step. Read it once from start to finish, then keep it to look things up.

> **This manual covers the mobile app only.** The web panel your office uses to set up teams, load content and decide what you detail has its own manual — see *Searle E-Detailing — Admin Panel User Manual*.

## 1. Quick start — your first day

If you read nothing else, read this.

| # | Do this |
|---|---|
| **1** | Open the app **with internet on**. Wait for *"Preparing today's calls…"* to finish. |
| **2** | Sign in with your **User ID** and **Password**. |
| **3** | Tap **e-Detailing** at the bottom. |
| **4** | Choose the **Call Type** — Chamber, Group or Walking. |
| **5** | Pick your doctor, tap **ARRIVED**, then **START DOCTOR CALL**. |
| **6** | Swipe through the slides. Stay on each slide for its minimum time. |
| **7** | Tap **Close**, fill the **Call Summary**, tap **Submit**. |

And the one rule that catches everybody:

> **Once you tap ARRIVED, either start the call or cancel it. Do not leave the screen.**

## 2. Before you start

The Searle E-Detailing app is what you use to:

- See the doctors assigned to you
- Show product slides to a doctor
- Record every call you make
- Record patients
- Check your own sales and call performance

**The first time you open the app, you need internet.** The app downloads your doctors, your products and all the slide images to your phone. You will see a screen saying **"Preparing today's calls…"**. Wait for it to finish.

After that first download, you can use the app **without internet**. See [section 17](#17-working-without-internet).

### The coloured bar at the top

Sometimes a thin coloured bar appears at the top of the screen. It tells you about your data:

| What it says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Syncing today's content… | The app is downloading new data | Wait. You can keep working. |
| Offline · showing data from [date] | You have no internet | Keep working. Your calls are saved and sent later. |
| Showing data from [date] · tap to update | Your data is from an older day | Tap the bar to download today's data. |

If no bar is shown, your data is up to date.

## 6. Doctors

This screen is your **doctor book**. You come here to look at doctor information. You **cannot** start a call from here — calls are started from the **e-Detailing** screen.

### What you see at the top

- **All Doctors** with a number — how many doctors are in the list right now.
- **"0 of 130 complete this month"** — how many doctors you have finished for the month.

### Filters

**Class** — shows only doctors of one class (A2, A4, and so on). Tap **All** to see everyone.

**Doctors** — four buttons:

| Button | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every doctor |
| **Visited** | Doctors you have called **at least once** this month, but are not finished yet |
| **Unvisited** | Doctors you have **not** called at all this month |
| **Completed** | Doctors whose monthly calls are **finished** |

> A doctor is either Visited, Unvisited or Completed — never two at the same time.

**Search** — type a doctor's name or specialty to find them quickly.

### The small circles on each doctor

Each doctor card has small circles on the right side. **One circle = one call you must make this month.**

- 🟢 **Green circle** = a call you have already made
- 🔴 **Red circle** = a call you still have to make

The number of circles depends on the doctor's class. An A4 doctor has 4 circles. An A2 doctor has 2 circles.

**Example:** an A4 doctor with 1 green and 3 red circles means you have made 1 call and 3 calls are still left.

### Opening a doctor

Tap **Details** to see the full record:

- **Professional Details** — hospital, address, city, last visit, class, PMDC number
- **This Month's Calls** — the circles again, plus how many calls are still owed
- A record of the calls you made this month — brands, SKUs and time spent

## 8. Content

These are the **slides you show to doctors**.

- Each card is one brand, with small pictures of its slides.
- The number at the top shows how many brands and SKUs you have.
- Tap a card to open the slides **full screen**.
- **Swipe left or right** to move between images.
- Tap the **✕** button to close.

This screen is only for viewing and practice. **During a real call the app chooses the slides for you** — the office decides which slides go with which doctor specialty, and you cannot change that from the phone.

## 9. The call types explained

Before every call you choose a **Call Type**. There are three.

| Call type | What it is | When you pick the doctor | What decides the slides |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chamber** | One doctor, in their chamber. Your everyday call. | **Before** — from your doctor list | The doctor you picked |
| **Group** | Several doctors together in one sitting. | **After** — you tick them in the summary | The **specialty** you choose before arriving |
| **Walking** | You walk in on a doctor without a fixed sitting. | **Before** — from your doctor list | The doctor you picked |

### Chamber

The normal call. You go to a doctor's chamber, you sit with them, you detail. You choose the doctor from your list before you start.

### Group

Several doctors at once — a ward meeting, a hospital session, a sitting where more than one doctor is present.

Two things make it different from a Chamber call:

1. **You choose a Specialty at the start, not a doctor.** That specialty decides which slides you show.
2. **You tick the doctors at the end**, in the Call Summary — after the detailing, not before.

That is the right way round: in a group you often do not know exactly who will sit in until it is over.

### Walking

You walk in on a doctor. The steps are **exactly the same as a Chamber call** — pick the doctor, arrive, start, detail, close. The only difference is how the call is marked on the record, so that the office can tell a walk-in from a scheduled chamber visit.

> **Chamber and Walking behave identically for you.** Do not worry about it during the call. Just pick the one that describes how the visit actually happened.

### And what about a joint call?

A **joint call** is when a manager sits in with you. It is **not** a fourth call type — you record it in the **Joint Call** field on the Call Summary at the end of the call, by naming who was with you (RM, SM, HOS or NSM). See [section 11](#11-the-call-summary-form).

## 10. Making a call (e-Detailing)

This is your main work screen. Tap **e-Detailing** at the bottom.

### Step 1 — Choose the call type

Three buttons under **CALL TYPE**: **Chamber**, **Group**, **Walking**. See [section 9](#9-the-call-types-explained).

---

### Step 2A — Chamber or Walking call (one doctor)

**1. Find the doctor.**
The list shows doctors who still owe you a call. Use the Class filter, the Doctors filter or the search box. Tap the doctor.

**2. Check the doctor's page.**
You will see their details, and notes from your last call if you wrote any. Read these before you go in — it is the fastest way to pick up where you left off.

**3. Tap ARRIVED.**
This records **where and when** you arrived. It works without internet.

> If the phone asks permission for location, tap **Allow**.

**4. Tap START DOCTOR CALL.**
This button only works after you have tapped ARRIVED.

**5. Show the slides.**
- Swipe left and right to move between slides.
- Each slide has a **minimum time**. Stay on the slide until that time is finished.
- The app counts how long you spend on each slide.
- **The back button will not work during a call.** This is on purpose.

**6. Tap Close** when the call is finished.
The **Call Summary** form opens. See [section 11](#11-the-call-summary-form).

---

### Step 2B — Group call (several doctors)

**1. Tap Group.**

**2. Choose the Specialty.**
The slides shown in the call depend on this. You must choose one before you can continue.

> Changing the specialty after you have arrived cancels the arrival and you will need to tap ARRIVED again. That is normal — the arrival belongs to the specialty you picked.

**3. Tap ARRIVED.**

**4. Tap START DOCTOR CALL.**

**5. Show the slides**, the same as a normal call.

**6. Tap Close.**
Now the summary asks you to **choose all the doctors who attended**. Tick every doctor who was there, then submit.

> **Important:** in a group call you choose the doctors **at the end**, not at the start.
>
> The list only shows doctors who have that specialty **and** still owe a call this month. If the list is empty, it means all those doctors are already complete.

---

### ⚠️ Very important

**After you tap ARRIVED, do not leave the screen.**

If you tap ARRIVED and then go to another screen without starting or cancelling the call, the app records it as a **cancelled call**. This is to make sure no visit is lost.

So after tapping ARRIVED, always either:

- Tap **START DOCTOR CALL**, or
- Tap **CANCEL CALL** and give a reason.

## 11. The Call Summary form

This form opens when you tap **Close** at the end of a call. It shows how long the call took and how many slides you showed.

Fill it in:

| Field | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Doctors** (group calls only) | Tick every doctor who attended. Required. |
| **Quick feedback** | Tap any that apply. You can choose more than one. |
| **Additional Comment** | Write anything else in your own words. Optional. |
| **Joint Call** | Choose **No** if you were alone. Otherwise choose who joined you — RM, SM, HOS, NSM. You can pick more than one. |
| **Samples Provided** | Choose the SKU you gave, or **None**. |

The quick feedback options are:

- Interested
- Need Follow-up
- Asked for Samples
- Price Concern
- Competitor Mentioned
- Next Visit Planned
- Prescribing Other Brands
- Plan Camp
- Requested for Activity

> **Joint Call note:** "No" and the manager names cannot both be selected. Choosing a manager removes "No" automatically, and vice versa.

> **Write a real comment.** Whatever you type here comes back to you as *notes from your last call* the next time you open that doctor. A useful line now saves you guessing next month.

Tap **Submit** to save the call.

## 12. Your call report

After you submit, the app shows a report of the call you just made:

- **How long** the call took
- **How many slides** you showed
- **Call Details** — call type, and which brands and SKUs you detailed
- **Time Spent per Brand** — seconds spent on each brand
- **Time Spent per Slide** — seconds spent on each slide
- **Post Call Analysis** — the feedback you wrote

For a group call, the report also lists every doctor who attended.

## 13. Cancelling a call

If you cannot do the call, tap **CANCEL CALL**. This button works after you have tapped ARRIVED.

Choose one reason:

- Doctor not available
- Doctor busy — no time
- Clinic closed
- Doctor refused to meet
- Waiting time too long
- Wrong / changed clinic timing
- Personal emergency
- Other

> If you choose **Other**, you **must** write the reason in the box.
>
> You may also add a note to any of the other reasons.

Tap the confirm button to save it. A cancelled call is still a record — it is not lost, and it does not count against you as a missed visit. It counts as a visit you made where the call could not happen.

## 14. Patients

Tap **Patients** on the Home screen. This is your **patient log** — the patients you have recorded.

- The list shows your patients, newest first.
- Use the search box to find a patient by name, doctor or city.
- Tap **Edit** on a card to change a patient you already saved.

### Adding a patient

Tap **+ Add Patient** and fill the form:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Patient Name** | Required |
| **Address** | Required |
| **City** | Choose from the list. Required |
| **Contact Number** | Required. Format +92-3XX-XXXXXXX |
| **Oral / PFS** | Choose one. Required |
| **Doctor** | Choose from your doctors. Required |
| **Speciality** | Fills automatically when you pick the doctor |
| **Indication** | Choose one. Required |
| **Dose** | Choose from your SKUs. Required |
| **Strength — Month 1** | Required |
| **Strength — Month 2 and 3** | Optional — fill only if the course continues |
| **Prescription** | A photo is required |

**Adding the prescription photo**

- Tap the add tile. Choose **camera** or **photo library**.
- You can add up to **5 photos**.
- Photos are made smaller automatically so they upload even on a slow connection.

Tap **Save Patient** to finish.

> Any field with a red star ( * ) must be filled. If something is missing, a red message appears under that field.

You can add a patient **without internet**. It is saved on your phone and uploaded later.

## 15. Analytics

Tap **Analytics** at the bottom.

### Choosing the period

At the top there are two boxes:

- **MONTH** — choose the month
- **DAYS** — choose the day range inside that month

By default it shows **this month, from the 1st until today**.

### Export PDF

Tap **Export PDF** to save or share a report.

> The PDF always contains **both** Call Performance and Sales Performance, no matter which one is on screen.

---

### Call Performance

Tap the **Call Performance** button.

**Calls Completed** — calls you finished in this period, next to the same period last month.

**The four boxes below:**

| Box | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Call / Planned** | Calls you made, out of calls you were supposed to make. The percentage is your **RFI**. |
| **Covered / Doctors** | How many different doctors you met, out of all your doctors. Meeting a doctor **once** counts here. |
| **Reach (Doctors Completed)** | How many doctors have **finished** all their monthly calls. An A4 doctor counts only after 4 calls. |
| **Avg Engagement Time** | Average time you spent showing slides per call. |

> **Call / Planned and Reach are different things.** Making 8 calls on one doctor raises Call / Planned but does not raise Reach. To raise Reach you have to finish doctors, not repeat them.

**The charts below:**

- **Avg. Engagement by Specialty** — average seconds per specialty
- **Avg. Engagement by Brand** — average seconds per brand
- **Time Spent per Slide** — seconds on each slide. Tap a specialty tab at the top of this chart to change specialty. The slides stay in the order you showed them.

---

### Sales Performance

Tap the **Sales Performance** button.

**Total Sales** — this period, the same period last month, and **GOLM** (your growth compared to last month).

**The three boxes below:**

| Box | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Monthly Target** | Your target for the month |
| **Achievement %** | How much of the target you have achieved |
| **Expected Sales for the Month** | Where your sales will land by month end if you continue at this speed |

**The charts below:**

- **Sales by Brand**
- **Sales by SKU**
- **Sales by Brick**
- **Top 10 Chemists** — your biggest customers, by value

> Each chart has a **Value / Units** switch. **Value** is money. **Units** is packs. You can set each chart separately.

## 17. Working without internet

The app is built to work in the field with no signal.

**Works without internet:**

- Seeing your doctors, portfolio and content
- Showing slides in a call
- Recording a call, including ARRIVED location
- Cancelling a call
- Adding a patient

**Needs internet:**

- The very first download after installing
- Getting today's updated doctors and content
- Sending your saved calls and patients to the office

**How it works:** everything you record is saved on your phone first. When your phone gets internet again, it is uploaded automatically. **Nothing is lost.**

> Your call counts and circles update straight away on your phone, even with no signal.

---

## 18. The rules that matter most

Six things. Everything else you can look up.

1. **Sync at the start of the day.** Settings → Offline Data → Sync now, while you still have signal.
2. **ARRIVED comes before START DOCTOR CALL.** The Start button does nothing until you have arrived.
3. **After ARRIVED, start or cancel — never just leave.** Leaving records a cancelled call.
4. **Stay on each slide for its minimum time.** The app is measuring, and short slides show up in your engagement figures.
5. **In a group call, the doctors are ticked at the end.** The specialty is chosen at the start.
6. **No signal is not a problem.** Keep working. It all uploads later.

## 19. Common questions

**The START DOCTOR CALL button does not work.**
Tap **ARRIVED** first. Start Call only works after that.

**I cannot go back during a call.**
This is on purpose. Finish the call by tapping **Close**, then fill the summary.

**A doctor is missing from the e-Detailing list.**
That doctor's monthly calls are probably finished. Tap the **Completed** filter to check.

**The doctor list is empty in a group call summary.**
It means every doctor of that specialty has already completed their monthly calls.

**My numbers look old.**
Look at the coloured bar at the top, or go to **Settings → Offline Data** and tap **Sync now**.

**I tapped ARRIVED and left by mistake.**
The app recorded a cancelled call so the visit was not lost. Go back to the doctor and do the call again.

**What is the difference between Chamber and Walking?**
Nothing changes for you — the steps are the same. Only the record of how the call was made is different.

**How do I record a call where my manager was with me?**
Do the call normally, then choose your manager under **Joint Call** in the Call Summary. There is no separate joint-call button.

**Why do some doctors have 2 circles and some have 4?**
It depends on the doctor's class. The class decides how many calls you must make in the month.

**Why can I not choose which slides to show?**
The slides are set by the office for each doctor specialty. You show what has been assigned. The **Content** screen is where you can look at everything and practise.

**I am seeing no slides in a call.**
Nothing has been assigned yet for that doctor's specialty. Tell your manager or the office.

**I forgot my password.**
Contact your administrator.
