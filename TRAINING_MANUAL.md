## How to use this book

Every lesson has three parts. Use all three.

| Part | What to do with it |
|---|---|
| **Do this** | Follow the steps on your own phone, one by one |
| **Check yourself** | Answer the questions out loud. If you cannot, do the lesson again |
| **Remember** | The thing people most often get wrong. Read it twice |

Do the lessons **in order**. Each one uses what you learned in the one before.

> **If you get stuck, ask your trainer straight away.** Do not wait until the end of the session. One small gap early on makes every later lesson harder.

**Total time:** about 3 hours, plus the practice day at the end.

## Before you begin

Check all of this **before** Lesson 1. Do not start without it.

| What you need | Why |
|---|---|
| **Your own phone, charged** | You must do every step yourself. Watching someone else teaches you almost nothing |
| **Your User ID and password** | Ask your manager if you do not have them |
| **Internet, for now** | The very first download needs it. After that you can work offline |
| **A practice doctor** | Your trainer will tell you which doctor to use in the lessons |

> **Important.** The calls you make in this training are **real records**. Use only the practice doctor your trainer gives you. Do not practise on a live doctor.

## Lesson 1 — Signing in and looking around

*15 minutes. By the end you can sign in and open any screen without help.*

### Do this

1. Open the app on your phone.
2. Type your **User ID**, then your **Password**. Tap the eye icon if you want to see what you typed.
3. Tap **Sign In**.
4. Wait for **"Preparing today's calls…"** to finish. The first time, this downloads all your doctors and slides onto the phone. It only happens once.
5. Look at the Home screen. Find your **name** at the top, and **today's date** under it.
6. Count the six cards: Doctors, Portfolio, Content, e-Detailing, Analytics, Patients.
7. Look at the four buttons at the bottom: **Home, e-Detailing, Analytics, Settings**.
8. Tap each of the four bottom buttons once, then come back to Home.

### Check yourself

- Without looking at your phone, name the four buttons at the bottom.
- Which of those four will you use most during your working day?

> **Remember.** The four bottom buttons never change. Wherever you get lost, tap **Home** and start again.

## Lesson 2 — Your doctors, and what the circles mean

*25 minutes. By the end you can say how many calls each doctor still needs this month, and why.*

### Do this

1. From Home, tap **Doctors**.
2. Look at the top. Note the number next to *All Doctors* — that is how many doctors are in the list right now.
3. Read the line under it, for example *"0 of 130 complete this month"*. This is how many doctors you have **finished**.
4. Look at one doctor card. Find the small **circles** on the right side.
5. Count the circles. **Each circle is one call you must make this month.**
6. Find a doctor with both colours. **Green** = done. **Red** = still to do.
7. Tap **Class**, then **A2**. Notice the list gets shorter. Tap **All** again.
8. Tap **Unvisited**. These are doctors you have not called **at all** this month.
9. Tap **Visited**. These are doctors you have started but **not finished**.
10. Tap **Completed**. These are doctors who are **finished** for the month.
11. Tap **All** again. Type a doctor's name in the search box to find them.
12. Tap **Details** on any doctor and read their **Professional Details**.

### Check yourself

- You open a doctor and see **1 green circle and 3 red circles**. How many calls have you made, and how many are left?
- What class is that doctor likely to be?
- A doctor is finished for the month. Under which of the four buttons will you find them?

> **Remember.**
> The number of circles comes from the doctor's **class**. An A4 doctor needs 4 calls. An A2 doctor needs 2 calls.
> A doctor is **either** Visited, Unvisited **or** Completed — never two at once.
> You **cannot start a call from this screen.** Calls start from **e-Detailing**.

## Lesson 3 — Your products and your slides

*15 minutes. By the end you know which brands you carry, and you have seen the slides you will show.*

### Do this

1. From Home, tap **Portfolio**.
2. Note how many brands you carry.
3. Pick one brand. Read the small grey pills under it — these are its **SKUs**.
4. Go back to Home. Tap **Content**.
5. Look at the bar at the top — it tells you how many brands and SKUs you have.
6. Tap any brand card. The slides open **full screen**.
7. **Swipe left and right** through the images. Look at every slide.
8. Tap the **✕** button to close.
9. Open two more brands and look at their slides. **Know your slides before you meet a doctor.**

### Check yourself

- What is the difference between **Portfolio** and **Content**?
- Can you change or delete a slide from your phone?

> **Remember.** Portfolio is your **product list**. Content is the **pictures you show**. Both are read-only — during a real call the app chooses the slides for you.

## Lesson 4 — Your first call (Chamber)

*35 minutes. By the end you can do a complete chamber call on your own, from arriving to closing.*

### Do this

1. Tap **e-Detailing** at the bottom.
2. Under **CALL TYPE**, tap **Chamber**.
3. Find your practice doctor. Use the search box.
4. Tap the doctor. Their page opens. Read their details.
5. Tap **ARRIVED**. If the phone asks for your location, tap **Allow**.
6. Look at the buttons. **START DOCTOR CALL** and **CANCEL CALL** are now active. Before you tapped ARRIVED, they were not.
7. Tap **START DOCTOR CALL**.
8. The slides open. Swipe to the next slide.
9. Stay on a slide for a few seconds. The app is counting your time on **every** slide.
10. Try to press the **back button** on your phone. Notice that nothing happens. This is normal.
11. Swipe through to the last slide.
12. Tap **Close**. The **Call Summary** opens. Leave it open — Lesson 5 continues from here.

### Check yourself

- You tapped START DOCTOR CALL but nothing happened. What did you forget?
- Why does the back button not work during a call?

> **Remember — this is the most important rule in the app.**
> After you tap **ARRIVED**, do not leave the screen.
> If you tap ARRIVED and then go somewhere else without starting or cancelling, the app records a **cancelled call**. This is so that no visit is ever lost.
> After ARRIVED, always do one of two things: **START DOCTOR CALL**, or **CANCEL CALL** with a reason.

## Lesson 5 — Filling the Call Summary

*20 minutes. By the end you can complete the summary correctly, without help.*

### Do this

1. Look at the top of the form. It shows how long your call took and how many slides you showed.
2. Under quick feedback, tap **Interested**. Notice it turns on.
3. Tap **Need Follow-up** as well. You can choose **more than one**.
4. Tap **Interested** again to turn it off. Tap it back on.
5. In **Additional Comment**, write one short sentence about the call in your own words.
6. Under **Joint Call**, leave **No** if you were alone. Now tap **RM** and watch **No** switch itself off.
7. Tap **No** again. Watch RM switch off. Only one answer can be true.
8. Under **Samples Provided**, choose a SKU, or leave **None**.
9. Tap **Submit**.

### Check yourself

- You were alone on the call. What do you choose under Joint Call?
- Your SM and your RM both joined you. Can you record both?
- Can you choose three quick feedback options at the same time?

> **Remember.** Fill the summary honestly — it is the record of your call, and your Analytics are built from it.

## Lesson 6 — Reading your call report

*10 minutes. By the end you can read the report the app shows after every call.*

### Do this

1. After you submit, your **call report** opens by itself.
2. Find **how long** the call took.
3. Find **how many slides** you showed.
4. Read **Call Details** — the call type, and the brands and SKUs you detailed.
5. Read **Time Spent per Brand** — seconds spent on each brand.
6. Read **Time Spent per Slide** — your time on each individual slide.
7. Read **Post Call Analysis** — the feedback you wrote a moment ago.
8. Go back to **e-Detailing**. Find your practice doctor and look at their circles. **One more circle is now green.**

### Check yourself

- Where does the app get *Post Call Analysis* from?
- You spent a long time on one slide and almost none on another. Where can you see that?

> **Remember.** Your circles update **immediately**, even with no internet. You do not have to wait for a signal to see your progress.

## Lesson 7 — A Group call

*25 minutes. By the end you can run a group call and record everyone who attended.*

### Do this

1. Tap **e-Detailing**. Under CALL TYPE, tap **Group**.
2. Notice the screen changes — there is **no doctor list** now.
3. Tap **Select Specialty** and choose one.
4. Tap **ARRIVED**.
5. Tap **START DOCTOR CALL**.
6. Show the slides, the same as a normal call.
7. Tap **Close**.
8. The summary now asks for **Doctors**. Tap it and tick **every doctor who attended**.
9. Fill the rest of the summary as you did in Lesson 5.
10. Tap **Submit**.

### Check yourself

- In a group call, when do you choose the doctors — at the start or at the end?
- The doctor list is empty when you try to tick attendees. What are the two possible reasons?
- Why must you choose a specialty before you can start?

> **Remember.**
> A group call picks its doctors at the **END**, not at the beginning.
> The list only offers doctors of the chosen specialty who **still owe a call** this month. An empty list usually means they are all complete.
> The **specialty** you choose decides which slides the app shows you.

## Lesson 8 — A Walking call

*5 minutes. By the end you know when to use Walking instead of Chamber.*

### Do this

1. Tap **e-Detailing**. Under CALL TYPE, tap **Walking**.
2. Notice the screen looks **exactly the same** as Chamber — the same doctor list, the same buttons.
3. Do not complete a call now. Tap **Chamber** again.

### Check yourself

- What is different between a Chamber call and a Walking call, for you?

> **Remember.** The steps are **identical**. Only the record of *how* the call was made is different. Choose the one that matches what you actually did.

## Lesson 9 — Cancelling a call

*10 minutes. By the end you can cancel a call properly, so the visit is still recorded.*

### Do this

1. Tap **e-Detailing**. Choose **Chamber** and open your practice doctor.
2. Tap **ARRIVED**.
3. Tap **CANCEL CALL**.
4. Read the list of reasons.
5. Choose **Doctor not available**. Notice you can now confirm.
6. Now choose **Other** instead. Notice you **cannot** confirm until you write something.
7. Write a short reason in the box.
8. Confirm the cancellation.

### Check yourself

- You choose *Other*. What must you do before you can confirm?
- Is a cancelled call lost, or is it recorded?

> **Remember.** A cancelled call is **still a record** — your manager can see that you went, and why the call did not happen. Cancelling honestly is always better than walking away from the screen, which records a cancellation with no reason at all.

## Lesson 10 — Recording a patient

*20 minutes. By the end you can add a patient with a prescription photo.*

### Do this

1. From Home, tap **Patients**.
2. Tap **+ Add Patient**.
3. Fill **Patient Name** and **Address**.
4. Choose the **City**, and type the **Contact Number** as `+92-3XX-XXXXXXX`.
5. Choose **Oral** or **PFS**.
6. Choose the **Doctor**. Watch **Speciality** fill itself in automatically.
7. Choose the **Indication**, and the **Dose**.
8. Fill **Strength — Month 1**. Leave Month 2 and Month 3 empty for now.
9. Under **Prescription**, tap the add tile. Take a photo, or choose one from your gallery.
10. Tap **Save Patient**.
11. Find your patient in the list. Tap **Edit**, add Month 2, and save again.

### Check yourself

- Which fields can you leave empty?
- You tap Save Patient and nothing happens. What should you look for?
- How many photos can you add?

> **Remember.** Every field with a red star ( \* ) must be filled, and a prescription photo is required. If something is missing, a red message appears **under that field** — scroll up and look for it.

## Lesson 11 — Working with no internet

*25 minutes. **This is the most important lesson in this book.** By the end you trust the app to work in the field with no signal.*

### Do this

1. **Before you switch anything off:** go to **Settings → Offline Data** and tap **Sync now**. Wait for it to finish.
2. Now turn on **flight mode**, or switch mobile data off.
3. Look at the top of the screen. A bar appears: *"Offline · showing data from [date]"*.
4. Open **Doctors**. Your doctors are still there.
5. Open **Content** and open a brand. The slides still open.
6. Go to **e-Detailing** and do a **complete chamber call** on your practice doctor, exactly as in Lesson 4.
7. Submit the summary. Notice the call is accepted normally.
8. Look at your practice doctor. **The circle turned green, with no internet.**
9. Now turn your internet back on.
10. Go to **Settings → Offline Data**. Look at **Last synced**.
11. Tap **Sync now**. Your call is sent to the office.

### Check yourself

- You are in an area with no signal all day. Can you still report your calls?
- Where do your calls go while you have no internet?
- What should you do every morning before you leave?

> **Remember.**
> Everything you record is saved on your **phone first**, then uploaded automatically when the signal returns. **Nothing is lost.**
> **Sync every morning** before you leave, while you still have internet. This gets you today's doctors and content.
> **Never reinstall the app or clear its data** while you have calls waiting to upload. Sync first.

## Lesson 12 — Reading your Analytics

*25 minutes. By the end you can read your own numbers and explain what each one means.*

### Do this

1. Tap **Analytics** at the bottom.
2. Look at **MONTH** and **DAYS** at the top. Change DAYS to 1–10 and watch the numbers change. Set it back.
3. Tap **Call Performance**.
4. Read **Calls Completed** — this period against the same period last month.
5. Find **Call / Planned** — calls made, out of calls owed. The percentage is your **RFI**.
6. Find **Covered / Doctors** — this counts a doctor once you have met them **even a single time**.
7. Find **Reach (Doctors Completed)** — this counts a doctor only when **all** their monthly calls are done.
8. Find **Avg Engagement Time** — your average slide time per call.
9. Scroll down. Read **Avg. Engagement by Specialty**, then **by Brand**.
10. Find **Time Spent per Slide**. Tap a different specialty tab above the chart and watch the bars change.
11. Now tap **Sales Performance**.
12. Read **Total Sales** and **GOLM** — your growth against last month.
13. Read **Monthly Target**, **Achievement %** and **Expected Sales for the Month**.
14. Scroll to **Sales by Brand**. Tap **Units**, then **Value** again. Units is packs; Value is money.
15. Scroll to **Top 10 Chemists** and read your biggest customers.
16. Scroll back to the top and tap **Export PDF**.

### Check yourself

- You made 8 calls this week, all on the same doctor. Which goes up — **Call / Planned**, or **Reach**?
- What is the difference between **Value** and **Units**?
- You export the PDF while looking at Sales. Does the file contain your call figures too?

> **Remember.** **Call / Planned** is about **calls**. **Reach** is about **doctors finished**. They move independently. Export PDF always contains **both** Call and Sales performance, whichever screen you are on.

## Lesson 13 — Settings and your password

*10 minutes. By the end you can change your password and check your data is up to date.*

### Do this

1. Tap **Settings** at the bottom.
2. Read your **Account Info** — name, role, username and team. Check they are correct.
3. Under **Change Password**, type your current password.
4. Type a new password of **at least 6 characters**. It must be **different** from your current one.
5. Type it again in **Confirm Password**, then tap **Update Password**.
6. Look at **Offline Data**. Read **Connection** and **Last synced**.
7. Tap **Sync now** and watch **Last synced** change.

### Check yourself

- What is the shortest password the app will accept?
- Where do you look to find out when your data was last updated?

> **Remember.** Do not share your password with anyone, including your manager. **Logout** is on this screen — do not log out in the field unless you have synced first.

## Practice day — a full run

*45 minutes. Do this **without** looking back at the lessons.*

This is a normal working day, in order. Work through it on your own. Ask for help only if you are truly stuck.

1. **Sync** your phone before you start.
2. Open **Doctors** and find one doctor who still owes calls.
3. Say out loud how many calls they still need, and **how you know**.
4. Turn your **internet off**.
5. Do a complete **Chamber** call on that doctor, with a real summary.
6. Do a **Group** call for one specialty, and tick two doctors as attendees.
7. Start a third call, then **cancel** it with a proper reason.
8. Record **one patient** with a prescription photo.
9. Turn your internet back on and **sync**.
10. Open **Analytics** and find how many calls you completed today.

> **If any step above stopped you,** go back to that lesson and do it again before the session ends. Do not leave the room with a gap.

## Am I ready? — final check

You are ready when you can do **all** of the following on your own, with nobody helping. Go back to the lesson for anything you cannot.

- Sign in and reach any screen without help
- Say how many calls a doctor still owes this month, and why
- Explain what the green and red circles mean
- Do a Chamber call from ARRIVED to Submit
- Do a Group call and tick the doctors who attended
- Say what happens if you leave the screen after tapping ARRIVED
- Cancel a call and give a proper reason
- Record a patient with a prescription photo
- Work a whole day with no internet, and sync afterwards
- Read your Call Performance and explain Call / Planned and Reach
- Read your Sales Performance and switch between Value and Units
- Change your password and check when you last synced

> **Still unsure about any of these?** Ask your trainer or your manager before you go into the field. Every one of them comes up in a normal working day.

## One-page reminder

*Keep this page with you.*

### The call, every time

**Choose call type → Pick doctor → ARRIVED → START DOCTOR CALL → Show slides → Close → Fill summary → Submit**

### The three rules

| Rule | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **After ARRIVED, never leave the screen** | Leaving records a cancelled call. Always Start or Cancel |
| **Sync every morning** | You get today's doctors and content, and yesterday's calls go to the office |
| **A group call picks doctors at the END** | Not at the start. Tick everyone who attended |

### The circles

One circle = one call owed this month.  **Green** = done.  **Red** = still to do.  The count comes from the doctor's **class**.

### If something looks wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| START DOCTOR CALL does nothing | Tap **ARRIVED** first |
| A doctor is missing from the list | They are finished — tap the **Completed** filter |
| Empty doctor list in a group summary | All doctors of that specialty are complete |
| Your numbers look old | **Settings → Offline Data → Sync now** |
| No internet all day | Keep working. Everything is saved and sent later |

> **Still stuck?** Look it up in the *User Manual*, then ask your manager or the support team.
